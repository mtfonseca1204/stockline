## Kora UI price copy

Never display "last published price", "latest published price", or variants such as "last published stock/equity price" in Kora UI, including tooltips, help, Activity, and the landing page. Do not surface price-source jargon. Keep numeric estimates optional and omit unavailable conversions. Use a simple "Estimated" qualifier when needed; never present a current estimate as a historical execution value. This rule applies to UI copy, not internal price data or financial calculations.
