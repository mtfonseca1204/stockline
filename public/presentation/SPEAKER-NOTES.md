## 2 · How it works · 0:22–0:48

“The flow has three steps. Deposit NVDAc into Morpho collateral. Review how much USDC is available, then confirm the borrowing action in your wallet. To exit, repay in USDC, or sell part of the collateral to repay. Once the position allows it, withdraw the remaining stock. Debt accrues interest, and falling collateral value can lead to liquidation.”

The moving dots illustrate the sequence. They are not transaction confirmations.
